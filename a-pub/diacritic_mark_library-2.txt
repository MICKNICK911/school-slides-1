DIACRITIC MARK LIBRARY
======================
Use this library with any image generator.
Format example:
  (text = "mat", letter = "a" at short-arc)
  (text = "all", letters = "all" at x-smile)
  (text = "go", letter = "o" at long-bar)
  (text = "which", letters = "wh" at smile-only)
  (text = "mat", letter = "a" at x-under)
  (text = "mat", letter = "a" at x-line)
  (text = "mat", letter = "at" at arrow-right)
  (text = "robot", between "o" and "b" at vertical-bar)
  single: (text = "mast", letter = "s" at star-above)
  multiple: (text = "mast", letters = "st" at star-above)
  single: (text = "mast", letter = "t" at underline)
  group: (text = "mast", letters = "st" at underline)
  (text = "flaunt", letters = "au" at x-between-smile)
  (text = "gent", letter = "g" at j-arch-top)
  (text = "something", letter = "o" at s-arch-top)





Only the specified letter(s) receive the mark. All other letters stay plain.

--------------------------------------------------
KEYWORD: short-arc
Exact visual description:
A single bright red concave arc (classic breve shape ̆). 
The arc is smooth, evenly curved, slightly thicker in the middle, 
with both ends gently lifting upward. 
It sits perfectly centered directly above the letter, 
hovering just clear of the top of the letter with a small, consistent gap.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000 or equivalent saturated red).
Size: proportional to the letter height – roughly 40-50% of the letter’s width.

KEYWORD: long-bar
Exact visual description:
A single straight, flat horizontal red bar (classic macron ¯). 
The bar is clean, uniform in thickness, with slightly rounded ends.
It sits perfectly centered directly above the letter, 
hovering just clear of the top of the letter with a small, consistent gap.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000 or equivalent saturated red).
Size: slightly wider than the letter itself, roughly 110-120% of the letter’s width.

KEYWORD: x-smile
Exact visual description:
Two stacked red marks placed directly beneath the specified letter(s) or letter group:
1. A small, clear red “×” (multiplication sign / cross) centered under the specified letter(s).
2. Immediately below the ×, a smooth red upward-curving smile / crescent 
   (soft U-shape or gentle smile). 
   CRITICAL: The smile MUST start exactly under the first specified letter 
   and end exactly under the last specified letter, spanning the full width 
   of the named group (e.g. for “ull” it begins under “u” and ends under the final “l”).
Both marks are pure vivid red (#FF0000).
They sit close together under the text with a small gap from the baseline of the letters.
The whole pair is horizontally centered under the specified letter(s) or group.

KEYWORD: smile-only
Exact visual description:
A single smooth red upward-curving smile / crescent (soft U-shape or gentle smile) 
placed directly beneath the specified letter(s) or letter group.
NO × or any other mark is present — only the smile.
CRITICAL: The smile MUST start exactly under the first specified letter 
and end exactly under the last specified letter, spanning the full width 
of the named group (e.g. for “wh” it begins under “w” and ends under “h”).
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000).
It sits with a small gap from the baseline of the letters and is horizontally centered under the specified letter(s) or group.

KEYWORD: x-under
Exact visual description:
A single plain red “x” (simple crossed lines, like a basic lowercase x or clean multiplication sign without heavy serifs).
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000).
CRITICAL POSITIONING: The x must sit EXACTLY and precisely under the named letter only. 
It must be horizontally centered on the vertical midline of that specific letter 
(not centered on the whole word). 
It sits close under the baseline of the letter with only a small, consistent gap.
No smile, no arc, no other marks — just the single red x under the specified letter.

KEYWORD: x-line
Exact visual description:
A thin, straight vertical red line that runs through the exact vertical center of the named letter.
The line continues downward and passes precisely through the center of a plain red “x” that sits under the letter.
The vertical line extends a short distance both above the letter and below the “x”.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000).
CRITICAL: The entire mark (vertical line + x) is locked exactly to the named letter only and can be applied to any single letter.
No other marks are present.

KEYWORD: arrow-right
Exact visual description:
A single straight horizontal vivid red arrow placed directly beneath the specified letter(s) or letter group, pointing to the right.
Structure:
1. Shaft: A clean, uniform-thickness straight horizontal red line, pure vivid red (#FF0000)
2. Head: A solid filled red right-pointing triangular arrowhead at right end
CRITICAL: MUST start exactly under first letter and end exactly under last, spanning full width (e.g. "ma" begins under left edge of "m" and ends under right edge of "a")

KEYWORD: vertical-bar
Exact visual description:
A single straight vertical vivid red line.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000).
Thickness: uniform, clean, slightly rounded ends.

CRITICAL POSITIONING:
- The line is placed BETWEEN two specified letters (e.g. between "o" and "b" in "robot"), NOT over or under a single letter.
- Horizontally, it must be centered exactly in the whitespace gap between the two letters.
- Vertically, it must extend from ABOVE the letters to BELOW the baseline:
    * Top reaches 40-50% above the top of the letters.
    * Bottom reaches 20-30% below the baseline.
- Thickness is roughly 4-6% of letter height.
- It must NOT touch either letter — small clear gap on both sides.
- No other marks — only the single vertical red divider.

KEYWORD: star-above
Exact visual description:
A small solid red asterisk / star (*) placed directly above a single specified letter.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000).
Shape: 6-pointed asterisk (*), clean, uniform, slightly bold.

CRITICAL POSITIONING:
- The star sits exactly centered ABOVE the named letter only, not between letters.
- It hovers with a small consistent gap clear of the top of the letter.
- When applied to multiple letters (e.g. letters = "st"), place one star above EACH specified letter, each centered on its own letter.
- Size is roughly 30-40% of letter height.
- No other marks — only the red star(s) above.

KEYWORD: underline
Exact visual description:
A single short straight horizontal vivid red bar placed directly beneath a specified letter or letter group.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000).
Shape: clean uniform thickness, slightly rounded ends, solid filled.

CRITICAL POSITIONING:
- The bar sits directly UNDER the named letter(s), not between or above.
- Horizontally, it must be centered exactly under the specified letter(s).
- It MUST start exactly under the left edge of the first specified letter and end exactly under the right edge of the last specified letter.
- For a single letter (e.g. "t"), it is slightly wider than the letter: ~110-120% of letter width.
- For multiple letters (e.g. "st"), it spans the full width from start of "s" to end of "t".
- Vertically, it sits with a small consistent gap below the baseline.
- Thickness is roughly 8-12% of letter height.
- No other marks — only the single red underline.

KEYWORD: x-between-smile
Exact visual description:
Combination of two vivid red elements: a smile arc under a letter GROUP + a small red "x" placed IN THE GAP BETWEEN two letters, in the middle.
Color: pure vivid red (#FF0000) for both.
Shapes: Smile = thick upward-curving arc, rounded ends. X = small clean "x".

CRITICAL POSITIONING:
- The smile MUST span the entire GROUP: start exactly under left edge of first letter, end exactly under right edge of last letter (e.g. from "a" to "u" in "flaunt").
- The "x" is NOT under a vowel. It sits centered HORIZONTALLY IN THE WHITESPACE GAP BETWEEN the two letters.
- Placement: middle of gap between "a" and "u", not aligned to either letter's center.
- Vertical layout: baseline -> small clear gap -> x in middle gap -> tiny gap -> smile below.
- Size: x ~25-30% of letter height. Smile thickness ~6-8% of letter height, length = full group width.
- DIFFERENCE FROM x-smile: x-smile = x under a vowel; x-between-smile = x between 2 letters middle.

KEYWORD: j-arch-top
Exact visual description:
Combination of two elements above a word: a small light-blue / periwinkle "j" and a thick vivid red arch behind it.
Color: j = light blue (#6C8AFF approx). Arch = pure vivid red (#FF0000).
Shapes: j = small lowercase j, clean. Arch = thick curved bar, slightly arched upward in middle, rounded ends.

CRITICAL POSITIONING:
- Applied ABOVE the first letter(s) of word (e.g. above "g" in "gent").
- The small blue "j" sits centered directly ABOVE the first letter, floating clear of baseline.
- The red arch sits directly BEHIND the "j", centered on the "j".
- The arch spans horizontally from above the first letter to above the second letter (e.g. from "g" to "e").
- The "j" overlaps / intersects the middle of the red arch, with the dot of "j" slightly above the arch.
- Layout top to bottom: dot of j -> arch crossing stem of j -> main word below with gap.
- No other marks — only small blue j + red arch above.

KEYWORD: s-arch-top
Exact visual description:
Combination of two elements above a word: a small light-blue / periwinkle "s" and a thick vivid red arch behind it.
Color: s = light blue (#6C8AFF approx). Arch = pure vivid red (#FF0000).
Shapes: s = small lowercase s, clean. Arch = thick curved bar, slightly arched upward in middle, rounded ends.

CRITICAL POSITIONING:
- Applied ABOVE the specified letter(s) of word (mirror of j-arch-top).
- The small blue "s" sits centered directly ABOVE the target letter(s), floating clear above.
- The red arch sits directly BEHIND the "s", centered on the "s".
- The arch spans horizontally from above the first letter to above the second letter.
- The "s" overlaps / intersects the middle of the red arch, sitting slightly above it.
- Layout top to bottom: s -> arch crossing behind s -> main word below with gap.
- No other marks — only small blue s + red arch above.




--------------------------------------------------
USAGE NOTES

- Always keep the original letter style (black, bold, clean sans-serif preferred).
- Marks are never applied to letters that are not explicitly named in the prompt.
- When multiple letters are specified, the mark is repeated under each named letter 
  or centered under the whole group if the group is treated as one unit (e.g. “all”).
- Do not add extra decorations, shadows, or outlines unless requested.

End of library.
